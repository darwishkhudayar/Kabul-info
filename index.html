Our Heritage

The History of Kabul

A city shaped by centuries of trade, culture, empires, and remarkable historical events.

Ancient Kabul

Kabul's history stretches back more than two millennia. Its strategic position made the region important to trade and cultural exchange across Central and South Asia.

Medieval Period

The city was influenced by successive kingdoms and empires, including periods associated with Islamic culture and regional trade networks.

16th Century

Babur, founder of the Mughal Empire, had a strong connection to Kabul and chose it as his burial place. His garden remains an important historical landmark.

19th and 20th Centuries

Kabul became central to Afghanistan's political development and gradually expanded into a modern capital.

21st Century

Kabul has faced conflict and major political changes while remaining an important center of Afghan culture, society, and national life.
